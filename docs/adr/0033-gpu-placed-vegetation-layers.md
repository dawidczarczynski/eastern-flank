# ADR 0033: GPU-placed vegetation layers – a generic cell-hash framework, first used by the shrubs

- **Status**: accepted
- **Date**: 2026-11-26
- **Task**: TASK-217a (extends ADR 0027 GPU-placed forest; builds on ADR 0024 chunked world, ADR 0026 real-scale vegetation)

## Context

ADR 0027 moved the forest impostors to the GPU: a chunk is a node sharing a MultiMesh with one instance per grid cell and no instance data, the vertex shader derives each tree from a 32-bit hash of its cell and the map textures, and the CPU plans nothing. The rest of the real-scale countryside – shrubs, grass, crops – was still planned per instance in GDScript (`ScatterChunks` bushes, `ScatterPlanner` edge shrubs and thickets, hedge and riparian shrubs, the grass planners): seconds of worker time after a camera jump and a fill-in the player sees. CLAUDE.md's performance budget now requires per-instance work that scales with map area to be hash-placed on the GPU. The forest code is specific to trees; three more layers (217a shrubs, 217b grass, 217c crops) need the same machinery.

## Decision

1. **One framework, three pieces**, all view-only and cosmetic (no `GameState.rng`, the game state never reads them):
   - `VegetationHash` (GDScript reference) + `assets/shaders/vegetation_hash.gdshaderinc` (GLSL twin): the cell hash, unit values, the jittered cell point, the instance → cell mapping and a value noise. The include **includes `forest_hash.gdshaderinc`** and reuses its uint32 hash, so forest and vegetation share one hash; a test fixes five (seed, cell) pairs and checks the include's constants.
   - `VegetationMasks`: the map as textures, built once per map at `TEXEL_M` = 4 m per texel (metres, through `MapScale`): an L8 **habitat** code per texel (meadow, field edge / roadside, heath, bank, hedge; 255 = **keep-off**: road and river ribbons with their clearance – the bridges lie on them – built-up land, open water; painted in rising code order with max()) and an L8 **forest edge** texture (ForestFill's stand mask box-blurred over 4-texel blocks: 255 inside, 0 outside, in between within ~10 m of an edge or clearing). Parcels stay `ParcelMap`'s texture; heights the lattice texture of ADR 0027.
   - `GpuVegetationLayer`: an `instant` ChunkLayer whose chunk is one `MultiMeshInstance3D` with the shared MultiMesh (bare for world-space quads, one shared identity buffer for real meshes drawn in local space), its cell range the instance uniform `cells`, culling bounds from the lattice heights. A layer may carry its own reach (`ChunkLayer.reach_m`) instead of a `ChunkRings` entry.
2. **Shrubs** (`ShrubRules` reference, `shrub_rules.gdshaderinc` twin, `ShrubField` on the camera-centred manager, `true` preset only): a 2.5 m grid (`shrub_cell_m`), at most one shrub per cell. Nothing on keep-off, in water, or inside a crop parcel – except its 3 m edge strip and the balks. The habitat (priority order) sets the chance and the kind mix: forest edge / clearing (~225 / ha), hedge (~1 per 2 m of line; the procedural strip hedges at the cells `plan_hedges` used, plus the real parcels' hedge lines), river / marsh bank (~140 / ha), heath (SCRUB / BARE land cover, ~80 / ha modulated by a 50 m value noise), field edge / roadside, and sparse noise patches on the meadow (≤ 10 / ha); none in the forest interior. **Four kinds**, each its own colour, silhouette and height range in metres: low bush 0.5–1 m (undergrowth model), hazel / blackthorn 1.5–3 m (shrub model, upright), juniper 0.8–1.5 m (spruce model, columnar; heath only), willow 2–3 m (shrub model, broad, silvery). Banks → ≥ 70 % willow, heath → ≥ 60 % juniper, hedges and forest edges → ≥ 60 % hazel.
3. **LOD split** (`LookPreset.vegetation()`): the real LOD0 mesh up to `shrub_mesh_range_m` 60 m (`shrubs_near`, 36 m chunks, `shrub_mesh.gdshader` = `foliage_body.gdshaderinc` under `PLACED_VEGETATION`: one surface per source species of the chunk's model variant, the juniper's only where the chunk has heath), impostor quads with the TASK-191 lit atlas from there to `shrub_impostor_range_m` 600 m (`shrubs`, 289 m chunks, `shrub_impostor.gdshader`), nothing beyond. Each shrub hands over at its own point of the `band_share` band. `GraphicsQuality` `shrub_range_m` caps the far end: HIGH 600 m, LOW 150 m (LOW builds no foliage today, so no shrubs at all; the cap applies if foliage is forced on).
4. **Migration**: under `true` `FoliageChunks.gpu_shrubs` drops every CPU shrub source (ScatterChunks' bushes, ScatterPlanner's edge shrubs and meadow thickets, the hedge and riparian shrubs; their trees stay), so no shrub is drawn twice. `balanced` is unchanged.

## Consequences

- (+) Shrub chunks exist the frame the camera reaches them; no worker time, no instance buffers; 217b / 217c reuse the hash, the masks and the layer.
- (+) The forest is untouched: `ForestField` keeps its own impostor layer (same pattern; it can move onto `GpuVegetationLayer` later), its TASK-189 fixed values are unchanged.
- (−) Rules exist twice (GDScript + GLSL), guarded by constant tests; headless tests see only the reference.
- (−) Vertex work for every cell of a near chunk times all its surfaces' vertices (~350–760 per cell), most of them collapsed; the shrubs' 0.7 ms GPU budget on the RX 580 must be measured in a playtest.
- (+) Shrubs stand on the ground as drawn: `NearHeightCache` (decision 2 of ADR 0027, now built) copies every built near-terrain chunk's own vertex heights into an RF window of 12 × 12 near chunks around the camera target (re-blitted and uploaded at most once a frame when a near chunk changed or the target moved on); elsewhere the lattice is what is drawn and what the shrubs read (sunk `shrub_sink_m` 0.1 m). Placement decisions read the lattice only, so the GDScript reference stays exact. On Suwałki the near mesh lies up to 3 m above the lattice (median 1.3 m at a forest edge): without the window most near shrubs were buried.
- (−) Masks read the land-cover raster blocky (no CoverLook noise). The ParcelMap image is not clipped by the cover (ParcelMap clips per query), so the habitat byte carries two flags from the raster – no real parcel here (forest, water, villages, marsh) / no strip parcel here (not FIELD) – and a crop texel blocks only where its flag allows a parcel; meadow parcels are no crop. Heath, crop and village borders are therefore ~16–20 m steps.
- (−) The habitat masks cost ~0.8 s at load on Suwałki (2346 × 1769 texels, GDScript ribbon rasterisation).

## Addendum (TASK-217b): near grass

The grass layer is the framework's second user. `GpuGrassField` is one `instant` GpuVegetationLayer (18 m chunks, build ring = `grass_range_m` 40 m, `reach_m` divided by the rings' `build_factor`) of identity instances, one per 0.8 m cell; its mesh has one surface per card (grass, flowers, reeds) and a cell's kind picks the surface that draws it. `GrassRules` / `grass_rules.gdshaderinc` reuse the shrub rules for where a clump may stand (habitat, crop interiors, keep-off: the shrubs' masks, height texture and `NearHeightCache` are shared) and add chance per habitat, bare-patch thinning, slope and shore rejection and the kind / height / flower / reed rules of `GrassPlanner` and `LookPreset.meadow()`. Decisions: (1) the old noises (sin-hash `TerrainNoise.fbm`) have no GLSL twin, so the rules use two-octave `VegetationHash` value noises at the same wavelengths in metres; bare patches therefore do not coincide with the terrain splat's dirt patches. (2) A flower replaces a clump in its cell with the old planner's share (`flower_density / PLAINS_DENSITY` x cluster noise); reeds are the bank habitat's clumps with probability `bank_reeds`. (3) The colour is the ground layers' average colours (grass, dry grass by the dry share), the near satellite hue and the kind colour; clumps thin out stochastically from 60 % of the ring to none at 40 m, while the terrain shader's grass tone (`grass_tone_*`, on under `true` on every quality, off under `balanced`) ramps in from 0.5 to 1.5 x the ring. (4) `GrassNear` is removed and `GrassField` plans nothing under `true`; `balanced` is untouched. (5) HIGH only (`gpu_grass`); there is no MEDIUM level. (6) Cost: every vertex of every cell of a near chunk re-evaluates the cell function for its surface (3 surfaces x 12 vertices); the 0.6 ms budget on the RX 580 must be measured in a playtest.
