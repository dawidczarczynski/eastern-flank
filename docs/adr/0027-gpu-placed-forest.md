# ADR 0027: GPU-placed forest – impostor trees derived in the vertex shader, not planned on the CPU

- **Status**: accepted
- **Date**: 2026-11-21
- **Task**: TASK-189 (builds on ADR 0024 chunked world, ADR 0026 real-scale vegetation)

## Context

At real size (ADR 0026) a forest hex holds ~2 000 trees. Each one is planned in GDScript on the worker threads (`ForestFill.plan_rect` + `TreeSpecies.pick` + `TreeImpostors.entry` + the MultiMesh buffer): ~50–80 µs a tree, ~100–200 ms per 2-unit impostor chunk, ~16 s of CPU to rebuild the impostor ring after a camera jump (measured on 4 WSL cores, TASK-135 playtest round 1). The playtest shows the consequence: after a zoom or a jump the forest fills in chunk by chunk over seconds (the canopy shell stands in meanwhile, but the player sees the swap). Micro-optimisations already took ~2.3x; the rest is the cost of interpreted per-tree work.

Everything that decides a fill tree is a pure function of its grid cell: a hash of (seed, cell) gives the jittered point, size, species roll and colour variation; the tile under the point decides forest / clearing; roads, rivers and field parcels keep it off; the ground gives its height. A vertex shader can evaluate the same function per instance, in parallel, every frame, for free compared with the CPU path.

## Decision

1. **Impostor trees are placed by the GPU.** An impostor chunk is a MultiMesh of the `TreeImpostors` quad with `instance_count` = the fill grid cells of the chunk and **no per-instance data**. `tree_impostor.gdshader` derives the cell from `INSTANCE_ID` and the chunk origin (instance shader parameter), and from it everything `ForestFill` / `TreeSpecies` / `TreeImpostors.entry` compute today; an instance whose cell holds no tree is collapsed to a point (as the LOD hand-over already does). Building a chunk is creating a node – no worker plan, no buffer.
2. **The map is handed to the shader as textures**, built once per map: a forest mask (tile is FOREST), a keep-off mask (roads, rivers, field parcels, the tile-centre clearing) at ~4.5 m, and the ground height (the lattice, plus a near-surface height tile cache around the target filled from the near terrain chunks' own height arrays as they are built – no extra surface queries).
3. **One hash, two implementations.** The integer hash and the cell → tree formulas exist once in GDScript (the reference, used by the full trees and by tests) and once in GLSL (`assets/shaders/forest_hash.gdshaderinc`). They use 32-bit unsigned arithmetic only, so both give the same bits; a test fixes the GDScript values of a few cells, the shader include carries the same constants, and a screenshot check confirms a full tree and its impostor stand at the same point.
4. **Full trees stay CPU-planned** (`ForestField` full layer, 170 m ring, ≤ 6 000 trees): few, near the camera, and they need the exact near-surface height. Their planning becomes cheaper still because the impostors no longer compete for the worker threads. **Superseded by the amendment of TASK-221 below: the full trees are GPU-placed as well.**
5. **Determinism and the simulation are untouched**: placement is cosmetic, uses no `GameState.rng`, and the game state never reads it.

## Consequences

- (+) An impostor chunk appears the frame its node is created: no seconds of filling in, no shell stand-in near the camera; the worker threads are free for terrain, grass and full trees.
- (+) Denser or farther impostors cost GPU vertex work only (collapsed instances are cheap), so the impostor range could grow towards the horizon and the canopy shell shrink.
- (−) The tree rules exist twice (GDScript + GLSL); a change to one must be mirrored, guarded by the fixed-value test and a screenshot.
- (−) Headless tests cannot see GPU placement: they test the GDScript reference and the mask / height textures, not the drawn trees.
- (−) Vertex work for every cell of a chunk, tree or not (a chunk is only created where it touches forest); to be measured on the RX 580 against the 16.7 ms budget.
- (−) The near-surface height cache is new state in the view layer (filled by `TerrainNear`, read by the shader), with its own resolution / memory limit.

## Implementation refinements (TASK-189)

- **No near-surface height cache.** The impostors stand on the drawn lattice height (an RF texture of the lattice vertices, interpolated in the shader exactly as `TerrainLattice.mesh_height_at`), sunk 0.5 m more than the full trees (was 1.5 m: the full trees popped up by that much at the hand-over) (`LookPreset.vegetation()` `impostor_sink_m`). Impostors start at ~230–250 m from the camera, where the lattice and the near surface differ by well under the sink; screenshots at distance 0.8–1.2 showed no floating impostors. The cache (decision 2) stays an option if a playtest shows floating or buried trees at the hand-over.
- **Every decision reads the lattice height** (shore, conifer share), in GDScript and GLSL alike, so the CPU and the GPU pick the same cells; only the drawn base differs (full trees: near surface).
- **Species from the integer hash.** The fill no longer uses `TreeSpecies.pick` (a `sin` hash, not reproducible on the GPU): `ForestFill` derives species, variant and colour variation from a third hash (`PICK_SALT`) with `ForestFill.unit`. `TreeSpecies.pick` itself is unchanged, so `balanced` and the planned trees are untouched.
- **One mask source (human decision, 2026-11-21: forests must not stay hex-shaped).** Where forest may stand comes from one small interface, `ForestMaskSource.build(grid, area, per_unit) -> {forest, keep_off}` (two L8 masks at a resolution the caller picks, independent of the hex). `ForestFill` merges them into one stand mask at 32 texels per unit (~4.5 m) that both its GDScript reference and `forest_impostor.gdshader` read; the shader knows no hexes. Today's source, `HexForestMaskSource`, fills the FOREST tiles row by row and keeps the tile-centre clearing and the road / river ribbons (widened by their clearance, near forest only) off – **transitional**: the forests are hex-edged until TASK-108g's sub-hex land-cover raster (~16 m) becomes a second source; then only the source changes, not the shader, the layers or the mask resolution. TASK-108g is a soft follow-up, not a prerequisite. Suwałki: the fill with its mask ~0.12 s, the whole impostor setup ~0.18 s at load. The parcels stay `ParcelMap`'s own texture.
- **Chunks own cells by centre** (`ForestFill.cells_of`): one shared MultiMesh (instances = the largest chunk's cells, no colour / custom data) for every chunk; the chunk's first cell and counts are an instance uniform; culling bounds come from `custom_aabb` over the chunk's lattice heights.
- **`instant` ChunkLayer**: no worker plan; new chunks are built on the main thread nearest first by the scan and then by `process()`, each within `instant_budget_usec` (4 ms; ~0.13 ms a chunk headless on Suwałki). After a ~2 km jump the target update takes ~3.8 ms and every impostor chunk within 1 km exists after the first `process()`. A layer `priority` lets the full trees plan before the near grass on the camera manager (otherwise ~20 s of near-grass plans delayed them after a jump).
- **Far thinning in the shader**: beyond 900 m from the camera every other cell is left out and the rest widen 1.4× (each tree at its own point of the band) – the CPU path did this per chunk by target distance; without it the GPU path cost +2–4 ms at distance 5–24.
- **Impostor yaw = full tree yaw**: the impostor picks its atlas view from the tree's own yaw (`k × 6`), so the hand-over shows the same side of the tree.
- **Burns** reach the impostors as a uniform array; the field keeps the last 128 circles (`ForestField.MAX_BURNS`, a battle leaves a few dozen wrecks) for full trees and impostors alike.

## Amendment (TASK-221, 2026-11-26): the full trees move to the GPU

Decision 4 gave two reasons for keeping the full trees on the CPU: they are few, and they need the exact near-surface height. Both are gone: TASK-217a built `NearHeightCache` (ADR 0033), the near mesh's vertex heights as a texture the shaders read, and the CPU planning still showed as trees filling in chunk by chunk after a jump or while panning (CLAUDE.md: per-instance work goes to the GPU). So the `forest_full` layer is now built like the impostors:

- **An `instant` layer of `GpuVegetationLayer`** (`FULL_CHUNK` 0.125 unit = 18 m chunks on the camera-centred manager; `ForestField.full_layer()`): a chunk is one `MultiMeshInstance3D` sharing one MultiMesh of identity instances (one per fill cell of the largest chunk, no per-chunk instance data), its cell range an instance uniform, bounds from the lattice heights plus the near surface's slack and the tallest tree. Nothing is planned: `ChunkManager.stats()` shows 0 plan ms for `forest_full`, and a chunk exists the frame the ring reaches it. Chunks without forest on the stand mask get no node.
- **One mesh, a surface per species.** The three variant models of a species are merged into one surface (the variant in the vertex colour's alpha, `ForestField._species_arrays`), each surface with its own species material (`forest_tree.gdshader` = `foliage_body.gdshaderinc` under `PLACED_VEGETATION` + `PLACED_LEAF_COLOR`: the LOD0 bark and cards, wind and tint as before). So a chunk is 5 draws, not 15.
- **The vertex shader derives the very tree of the impostor** from the cell: the same point, species, variant, scale, yaw, hue / value / saturation and phase (`forest_cell.gdshaderinc` holds the fill uniforms, the lattice lookup and the stand test the impostor shader used to carry itself; `forest_hash.gdshaderinc` the hash). Cheap tests come first, per vertex: the pick hash gives variant and species, which fail for ~90 % of the vertices of a cell (a cell is drawn by one of the 15 species × variant models and collapsed by the other 14); the lattice and the stand mask are read only for the rest.
- **Base height: the drawn near surface** (`near_height.gdshaderinc`, shared with the shrubs and the grass; `RealScaleVegetation` owns the one `NearHeightCache` window and hands it to the forest and the shrubs), sunk by the tree sink. Where no near chunk is built the lattice, as the shrubs.
- **Hand-over with the impostor: exactly one draws every tree.** The band is the impostor's: the full tree is drawn while the camera distance to the *impostor's* base (lattice height under the impostor's sink) is below `min(mix(centre (1 - band_share), centre (1 + band_share), phase), full_reach)`, the very expression and distance `impostor_in_band` tests for the impostor.
- **The budget is gone.** `full_tree_cap` and `VegetationRanges.budget_reach` are removed (ADR 0026 decision 3): the count of trees no longer costs CPU, and the GPU cost of a cell is its vertices (7 323 over all 15 models, ~500 per model), whatever the forest density. The cost limit is `LookPreset.vegetation()["full_tree_range_m"]` (45 m): full trees are drawn only that far from the camera, each at its own point of the `band_share` band, and the impostors take over (the layer's `forest_full` entry in `chunk_rings.tres` is gone). Measured on the RX 580 (HIGH, camera in a forest): at the old ~255 m range the layer cost ~35 ms, because every cell costs the vertices of all 15 models; at 45 m ~3 ms (cost grows with the square of the range). A longer range needs compaction of the live cells in a compute pass (`multimesh_get_buffer_rd_rid`), not done here. `full_reach` is still the nearest distance of a full chunk that is not built (`ForestField._unbuilt_reach`, now over the built-chunk set): beyond it the impostors draw, tree by tree.
- **Reference twin**: `ForestField.full_trees(chunk)` (ForestFill.cell_tree of the chunk's cells on `drawn_ground`, with the transform `_fill_transform` the shader mirrors); fixed cell values stay in `test_forest_fill`, expression checks of both shaders in `test_real_scale_vegetation`, the layer's contract in `test_forest_full_trees`.
- `balanced` is untouched (`TreeField`'s CPU LOD path).

## Amendment 2 (TASK-221 follow-up): compute compaction of the full trees

Playtest of the amendment above: with the range at 45 m the forest was full 3D only at the closest zoom, the screen edges at a high zoom showed the impostors ("eating" of trees while panning), and the impostors flickered while the camera moved (the build ring of the full chunks was exactly the range and the manager rescans after a quarter of the ring, so unbuilt chunks inside the range capped `full_reach` at their distance). The human chose compaction in a compute pass over a shorter range.

- **No chunk layer, no vertex-derived trees.** `ForestFullTrees` (`src/presentation/forest_full_trees.gd`) runs `assets/shaders/forest_cells.glsl` every frame (the camera moves): one thread per fill cell of a square window around the camera derives the cell's tree exactly as the impostor and the old vertex shader did (same hash include, lattice, stand mask, near surface, burns, per-tree hand-over band judged by the impostor's base) and appends its transform and variation (`INSTANCE_CUSTOM`: hue, value, saturation, phase) to the list of its model (species x variant, 15 lists) with an atomic counter. The lists are copied (`RenderingDevice.buffer_copy`) into the buffers of 15 MultiMeshes (`RenderingServer.multimesh_get_buffer_rd_rid`), one per model, drawn with the species material (`forest_tree.gdshader` = `foliage_body.gdshaderinc` + `COMPACTED_TREES`, which collapses an unused slot, a zero basis, before any work). A tree costs the vertices of its own model only: the 15x waste of ADR 0027 decision 4 is gone.
- **Capacity and draw count.** A list's capacity is the worst case of a forest on every cell within the species' range (cells x share x 1/3 + 5 sigma). The draw count (`visible_instance_count`) follows the list's count of a few frames ago (`buffer_get_data_async`) x 1.3 + 16, so the unused capacity is not drawn. The lists are zero-cleared every frame.
- **Range follows the view** (second playtest: trees still switched to impostors at the screen edge while moving into the forest). The full trees reach the far edge of the viewport: `RealScaleVegetation.set_view` -> `ForestField.set_full_range` takes the distance to the ground along the farthest of the top corner / middle rays (x 1.1), clamped to [45 m, `full_tree_range_m` = 250 m]. Every species uses that range (the species' own full ranges are out of the way: the impostor shader gets `NO_CAP` for them, `full_cap` is the range, `near_cull` follows it), each tree switching at its own point of the band, i.e. at the screen's edge or beyond. Capacity is sized for 250 m. Cost is the real number of trees in range.
- **No GPU, no full trees.** Without a `RenderingDevice` (headless, Compatibility) `ForestFullTrees.gpu()` is false, nothing is drawn and the impostor material's `full_cap` is 0 (impostors from the camera).
- **Reference twin**: `ForestField.full_trees_around(camera)`; `test_forest_full_trees` checks the reference, the capacity, the parameter block layout and that the shader compiles (glslang at import). `FOREST_FULL_VERIFY=1` (forwarded by `tools/screenshot.sh --windows`) prints what the pass does.
- Removed: the `forest_full` chunk layer, `FULL_CHUNK`, `ForestField.full_layer()/reach()/full_nodes()`, the placed-tree vertex path of `forest_tree.gdshader`, `_species_arrays`, the `full_reach` of the full trees (the impostors' `full_reach` stays at its no-cap default).
