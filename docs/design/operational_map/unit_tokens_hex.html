Realistic formation counters and installation markers – tilted staff map

Area: operational map · accepted direction: D, all the same data and states as the accepted counters

1 · The need

The extruded slab (and its cartoon frames) reads as a game icon, not as part of a realistic map. The new look must feel like something that belongs on a tactical map: no heavy outlines, no chunky 3D, material and light instead of borders. Rules kept from TASK-125 / 092b: screen-space, fixed pixel size at every zoom, side shown by shape (own rectangle · enemy diamond) and colour, strength gauge of 5 slanted segments, APP-6 glyph, same states. The same family is used for installations (depot, airfield, air defence, command post) so nothing looks foreign.

  • Pitch rule (kept): at pitch 0 (flat map) every variant falls back to the flat plate of the accepted style A (below); the 3D parts fade in with the pitch up to 35°.
  • Light: upper left (hillshade 315°); soft contact shadow lower right; no outlines except for states (hover = ui.text, selected = amber brackets, contact = dashed ui.alert ring on the ground).
  • No new tokens: every colour is a side colour, map.void / ui.* mixed, or neutral white / black at an alpha.

FLAT FALLBACK (pitch 0, all variants) – today's accepted plate

2 · Variant D – hex game tile with a raised side plate accepted

A thin hexagonal wargame token in matte painted resin: bevelled rim, a recessed field and the NATO symbol engraved (dark line with a light edge), the five strength pips as small round studs under it – like the reference. It lies on the ground, viewed at a fixed oblique angle in screen space (flat top view at pitch 0, ~20 % foreshortening and 5 px of thickness from the 35° tilt on), so it is a physical object without any outline. The hexagon is only the base, identical for both sides (neutral matte, flat-top); on it stands a slightly raised plate that carries the side by shape and colour: own = rectangle with the cut corner, enemy = diamond, in the side colour, as in the accepted counters. Stacks are literally stacked tokens. Installations are the same hex base, smaller, with a chamfered square plate and four studs.
