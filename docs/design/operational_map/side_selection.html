The need

Today Operation in the main menu jumps straight to the staff map and the side comes from --side= or the first turn. The human must choose which side to command (NATO or Russia, data/sides/*.tres) before the map opens. The screen shows per side: flag, name, lead faction, one briefing line, alliance decision, whether nuclear use is available (may_use_nuclear) and the scenario facts (operation, map, seed); buttons BACK (to the main menu) and START. The pick is passed as the staff map's viewer_player (the other side becomes ai_side); No hot-seat option (decided 2026-11-12; --hotseat stays a command-line flag). States: nothing picked (START disabled) · hover · picked · keyboard focus. Meaning by shape and text, not colour: picked card carries "◆ YOU COMMAND" tag; the opponent card is labelled "OPPONENT · AI". Palette has only own / enemy – the picked side is drawn own blue (rectangle), the other enemy red (diamond); NATO / Russia have no colour of their own, so the picked blue changes when you switch. Briefing texts are sample wording.
