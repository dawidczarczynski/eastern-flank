Unit info: a popup at the counter, the full panel on demand

The problem. Selecting a formation opens the 300 px StaffMapFormationPanel docked at the screen edge. Since it moved to the left it covers the north-west of the theatre – the player cannot click Kaliningrad to give a march order. A panel that opens on every selection will always cover something.

The idea (all variants). Split the information in two levels, as in the tactical battle:

  • Glance – a small card next to the counter (hover and/or selection). It never takes mouse input (pointer-events: none), so a click on a hex under it still lands on the map. It never covers the counter or its route.
  • Full – the existing 300 px docked panel, opened only on purpose: right-click on a counter, an INFO slot in the radial ring, or key I. Closed with ✕ / Esc / I. Content and frame stay as accepted (companies list, location, country…).
Interaction conflict to settle. Today a right click on a hex with an own formation selected opens the order ring (March / Attack / Defend / Cancel, keys 1–4). This proposal adds: right click on a counter = unit info (own: ring with INFO, enemy: full panel directly). Right click on any other hex stays the order ring, which gets [5] INFO too, so the way to the full panel is always the same slot.

A · Tag, brief card, full panel ACCEPTED

Closest to the accepted components (frame = notice card, rows = formation panel rows, ring = existing RadialRing). Hover shows a one-line tag; selection shows a brief card; both are click-through.

Recommendation and open questions

  • A: the brief card gives the player the numbers they actually need while ordering (strength, posture, current order) right where they look, and click-through plus the anchor rule removes the Kaliningrad problem. B is the quietest alternative; C is the fallback if floating cards still annoy.
  • No NEW TOKEN in any variant; everything is PANEL_STRONG, PANEL_HINT, LINE, HUD_*, side.own/side.enemy, ui.amber.
  • Colour-blind: side is carried by the tab text ("OWN" / "HOSTILE"), not only by colour; estimated enemies read "EST.".
  • Decided 2026-11-13: RMB on an own counter opens the ring with INFO; the full panel follows the selection while open and a click on the bare map closes it; the tag on a hostile counter shows only what intel allows. Open: (1) RMB on own counter: ring with INFO (A, B) or full panel directly (faster, but no ring on the counter)? (2) Should the full panel follow the selection while open? (proposed: yes). (3) Hover tag on enemy counters shows only what intel allows (fog, TASK-100) – confirm.
