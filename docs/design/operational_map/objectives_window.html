ACCEPTED 2026-11-17 · variant A (B and C rejected) with the main / side split · TASK-175

Objectives window and objectives map layer

Source: the human's playtest of TASK-165 (screenshot, 2026-11-17): the objectives window is unreadable. Asked for: a redesign of the window, an objectives layer on the map (toggleable), and click an objective in the window → the map centres on it and highlights it.

1 · What is wrong today (from the screenshot)

  • No hierarchy. Every row is the same size; name, points and holder compete; the Gap row looks like the others.
  • The rule is repeated four times ("Scored at the end, round 30" under every hex objective) in 10 px mono muted grey – noise, and still hard to read. All four hex objectives share one rule; it should be said once.
  • Colour alone carries the holder. Green / red words use the HP colours (not the own blue / enemy red of the map) and nothing but the word differs. Accepted rule: shape + word carry meaning, colour is the second channel (own rectangle, enemy diamond, neutral circle).
  • Gap progress is a tiny amber text line – the most important rule (win at once after 2 rounds) is the least visible.
  • Nothing connects a row to the map: the player cannot see where the objectives are.

The "scores for" line is shown only for the viewer’s own objectives (enemy rows show the points muted with the word ENEMY). Layer key M, default on; names on the map from Z1, the selected one always. 7 · Recommendation and open questions

  • New tokens: none. Deviation from today: holder colours change from HP green / red to side.own / side.enemy + shapes; window width 330 → 420.
  • Open: (1) layer key M and default on? (2) show the objective name on the map always or only from Z1 (proposed: from Z1; the selected one always)? (3) in variant A, show "scores for NATO / Russia" for every row (proposed) or only the viewer's own?
