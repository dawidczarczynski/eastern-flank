Terrain control on the operational map

1 · The need

Today: the map shows the country tint (stance, ~5 %), the dash-dot state border and the front line with a 5 % tint of "who is nearer". That tint is derived from the formations only (FrontLine, a display). It does not show HexControl – the real rule: a hex changes holder when a formation ends its move there or wins the battle there. So captured ground is invisible: after an advance you cannot see what you or the enemy took.

Says: which hexes are held by a side that is not the country's original side (occupied / captured), and by whom. When: always on at Z0–Z2 (toggle key, default on). Data: HexControl.holder(hex) != initial side of the hex's country (hex_holders vs hex_initial). Must not hide: counters, route ribbon, place names, border line, front line teeth. Meaning by shape, not only colour: own = blue, enemy = red, and the occupation pattern / contour differs from the plain country tint.

Frames below (simplified backdrop, viewer = NATO / own blue): country border dash-dot in the middle; left = own country (blue 5 % tint), right = enemy country (red 5.5 % tint). In the south-east, own forces hold enemy hexes; in the north-west, the enemy holds own hexes. Counters are shown as simple hex tiles.

2 · Common to all variants

  • Layer below counters, route, orders and labels, above the terrain and the country tint (the holder colour replaces the country tint on an occupied hex). mouse_filter IGNORE; rebuilt only on control_changed / objective_changed; contour smoothed like the front line (midpoints + Chaikin ×3), exact at Z2 grid.
  • Toggle: key K (free: P political, F front, O orders, T terrain, R rail, G grid, L labels, N names, B borders, C air defence); seventh row in Map options (below), default on.
  • Hex card (hover) gets the existing "control" row filled: CONTROL Russia – occupied (PL) / NATO – held / NATO – captured from Russia.
  • Colour-blind: the pattern / contour shape says "occupied"; the side colour says by whom, and the card says it in words.
