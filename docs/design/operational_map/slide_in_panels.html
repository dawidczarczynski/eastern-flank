Slide-in side panels

The large sections – strike, politics, journal – stop being windows over the map. They slide in from a screen edge between the turn bar and the bottom bar, and the map is resized to what is left (the map area is the "clear zone" of TASK-150, now simply the free rectangle). Small things stay overlays: confirm cards, report cards and chips, hex / unit cards, tooltips, the order ring. The full-screen sheets (Decisions, Escalation ladder) stay as they are. Panels reuse the accepted docked-window frame (PANEL_STRONG, 1 px LINE, chamfers 0,16,0,16, side-coloured tab, X 32 px). Frames below are 1440×900 at 47 %; the map scene is drawn schematically (Lida airfield in the east is the case from TASK-162).

Common behaviour. Slide 200 ms ease-out, the map viewport narrows in the same 200 ms and the camera keeps the centre of the visible map fixed (no jump); closing reverses it. Keys S / H / J and the bar buttons toggle, Esc and X close, the lit bar button shows what is open. Minimum map width 600 px: opening a panel that would go below it closes the panel on the same side or, if none, refuses with a hint. With a panel open, a click on the bare map no longer closes it (the map is not covered any more) – only X, Esc or the key. The formation panel (300 px) keeps its TASK-150 behaviour for now and is not part of this change. The clear-zone insets of TASK-150 / TASK-162 become the panel widths, so the strike-panel special case goes away.
