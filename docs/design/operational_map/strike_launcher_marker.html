Strike launcher marker

The need

When a missile is launched, the staff map shows what fired it at the launch point (strike_launched.origin, TASK-147): fighter, bomber (both fly over, see "Aircraft flyover"), ship, submarine (kind exists, no weapon yet), truck launcher (TEL), drone site. It stays for the flight of the salvo (longest trace, TASK-141b), then fades in 0.6 s, never outliving the strike toast. Label = the means' platform text ("truck launcher (HIMARS)"), only at the zoom levels where counters are labelled. Presentation only: not selectable, no hit-testing, up to 8 markers, same-hex markers stack with an offset, one CanvasItem, redraw only while visible.

Rules for all variants: fixed screen size (like counters / place names), both sides (own = rectangle-cut plate / blue, enemy = diamond plate / red) – side by shape plus colour, the kind by silhouette (never colour alone), no new tokens, polygons only (no assets). Sits below counters and the trace lines, above terrain. Never drawn over the selected counter's brackets (it is a 12 px offset up-left from the launch hex centre when the hex also holds a counter).

Silhouettes (shared by all variants)

Revised 2026-11-12: swept-wing fighter with a canopy cut-out, heavy four-engine bomber with long, nearly straight wings and a tailplane, frigate with gun / mast / radar, submarine with sail, periscope and dive planes, truck with canted canisters and cab, delta drone on a launch rail. Shown at 72 px and at the real 28 px.

Side-on / top-down simplified shapes, one filled polygon set each, 24×24 design box, nose to the right, drawn in a single colour. Unknown kind → plain dot.

Variant A – hex tile with a ghost plate (closest to the accepted tokens)

Reuses the installation tile of unit_tokens_hex.html (hex base R 18, recessed field, bevel) with the side plate (own cut-corner rectangle, enemy diamond) carrying the engraved silhouette instead of an APP-6 glyph. The tile is drawn at 85 % opacity and a thin dashed ground ring marks "temporary, not a unit". Name tag to the right in the place-name style. No integrity studs.

Variant B – bare silhouette with a launch tick (ACCEPTED)

No tile. The silhouette itself is the marker: 28 px, filled in the side colour with a 2 px map.void halo for contrast on any terrain, standing on a small launch tick (a 6 px ring on the exact launch hex centre, where the trace starts). Side is carried by a mini side chip (7 px rectangle / diamond) at the glyph's top-left, so colour is never the only channel. Label under the glyph, centred. Light, reads as "a thing that just happened", clearly not a unit or an installation.

Aircraft flyover (fighter, bomber) – added 2026-11-12 after playtest

An aircraft is not parked at the launch point. It appears ~230 px behind it (away from the target, fades in 250 ms), flies straight to it facing the target (1.8 s; the glyph is rotated to its heading), the missiles leave when it is over the launch tick (the approach replaces the 1.2 s lead-in), then it banks away in a half-circle U-turn to one side (1.2 s, heading turns 180°) and fades out over the last 0.6 s. The launch tick and the label stay at the launch point through the flight of the salvo. Several launches from one hex: turn sides alternate and the approach lines are shifted ±12 px. Ships, submarines, truck launchers and drone sites keep the static marker.

Godot notes: draw in StaffMapStrikeLayer with draw_colored_polygon / draw_polyline (halo = polyline of the outline), glyph polygons in a pure LauncherGlyphs; pure StaffMapLauncherMarker.alpha_at(t, flight) for the lifetime test. Pros: lightest to draw, instantly distinct from unit tiles, kind is the first thing seen. Cons: less "built" than the tile language; relies on the halo for contrast.

Variant C – label chip only

Minimal: a one-line chamfered chip at the launch point (silhouette 14 px + platform text) in the style of the strike chip. No tile, no tick: the chip's left edge is the launch point. Works at every zoom (the glyph stays; only the text hides on Z0).

Recommendation and open questions

  • B – the silhouette is the information (kind at a glance, 28 px), light to draw (8 markers, 60 FPS), cannot be mistaken for a unit or an installation. A is the choice if you want everything on the map to share the tile language.
  • New tokens: none. All colours are existing tokens (side.own, side.enemy, map.void, ui.text, PANEL_STRONG).
  • Open: (1) should the marker also show for the enemy's launches the viewer cannot see (fog)? Proposed: only launches the viewer sees (as the trace and the report), (2) pop-in and tick pulse OK, or fade only? (3) submarine has no weapon yet – keep the shape ready?
