## Verdict

- **Direction:** dark, muted, **flat top-down with baked relief** (orthographic camera, hillshade from the DEM burnt into the base texture, soft-light ~0.8). 2.5D (~34° tilt) is shown and rejected as the default – it compresses the north (~40 % fewer pixels per hex), makes arrows and the front uneven and needs billboard counters; suggested only as an intro animation / "briefing" mode. Light paper map and full 3D rejected as well.
- **Conflict with our direction change (GDD, 2026-11-07: "modern, slightly 3D welcome"):** the *modern, no paper* part is confirmed by the proposal; the *slightly 3D* part is not – the designer argues for flat. **Decided by the human (2026-11-07): 2.5D is wanted** – [TASK-125](../../tasks/TASK-125-staff-map-restyle-2-5d.md) implements the proposal's look with a tilted camera (default ~35°, `V` toggles flat) and answers the designer's objections (pixel compression, uneven lines, billboard counters).
- Everything is consistent with HUD v3 (Chakra Petch, JetBrains Mono, amber `#e8b04a`) and with the architecture rules (view reads state only, counters on a `CanvasLayer`, one palette resource).

## Key values

**Palette** (one `MapPalette` resource, key names as below; there is no NATO / Russia colour, only own / enemy):
`map.void #0b0d0b` · `map.land #1d211c` · `map.forest #22302a` (edge `#2b3d31`) · `map.urban #363931` (edge `#4b4e45`) · `map.marsh #46677a` (7×5 px hatching) · `water.sea #0f1820` · `water.lake #132029` · `water.shore #2b4454` · `river.major #5384a0` / `named #46728c` / `minor #3a5c70` · `road.t1 #b4ac98` (outline `#0d100d`) / `t2 #7f7a6c` / `t3 #55534a` · `rail #70746b` · `border #a9afa1` · `side.own #5b93d6` (ink `#0f1a26`, country tint 5 %) · `side.enemy #d76a52` (ink `#2a110b`, tint 5.5 %) · `side.neutral #a8a598` · `ui.amber #e8b04a` · `ui.text #dfe5d6` · `ui.muted #8d9585` · `ui.alert #e07c5e` · strength `#b9d38a` (≥75 %) / `#e2c069` (≥50 %). Water labels `#6f95ab`, label halo `#111411` 3 px.

**Line hierarchy** (screen px, constant under zoom; Z0 / Z1 / Z2): front 2.4 / 3 / 3 (+ teeth 5.5 px every 15 px) · state border 1.4 dash-dot (casing 3.6) · road t1 1.3 / 1.9 / 2.4 · major river 1.8 / 2.2 / 3 · rail – / 1 / 1 · road t2 – / 1.3 / 1.6 · named river – / 1.4 / 1.8 · minor river – / – / 1.2 · road t3 and dotted drainage canals Z2 only 0.9. OSM mapping: motorway / trunk / primary → t1, secondary / tertiary → t2, rest → t3; rivers > 40 km with a name → major / named. Draw simplified OSM geometry (Douglas–Peucker ~150 m), not hex centres. One line mesh per class, built once; zoom changes only width (shader uniform `px_per_world`) and visibility.

**Zoom levels:** Z0 < 1.6 px/km (countries, capitals + 2 cities, t1 roads, 4 rivers, lakes > 3 km, front, brigade counters ×n) · Z1 1.6–5 px/km (+ towns, t2, rail, named rivers, battalions, orders) · Z2 > 5 px/km (+ villages, t3, canals, hex grid 7 %; `G` forces the grid at 12 %). Fade 150 ms with ±10 % hysteresis. Hillshade az 315°, alt 38°, ×1.4 on Z0; optional 25 m contours at Z2. Towns and lakes drawn from real outlines, not single hexagons.

**Typography:** Chakra Petch (country names 26–34 px, tracking .42em, 55–60 % stance colour; titles) · IBM Plex Sans Condensed (places: capital 13 px caps, city 12.5, town 11, village 10, water italic) · JetBrains Mono (data on the map). Label collisions: rank, then distance to cursor; counter-colliding labels try E, W, NE, SW, else hide.

**Counters** (fixed screen size, on a `CanvasLayer`): own rectangle 40×26 px, enemy **diamond** 38×38 px (shape encodes the side, colour is the second channel); recommended style A "HUD" (side fill, ink glyph, cut corner); B classic APP-6 and C outline as alternatives. Strength = 5 slanted segments under the frame (HP colours). Estimated enemy: dashed frame, "?", "EST.", no strength bar. States: normal, selected (2 px corners), has order, moved, in contact (dashed `ui.alert` ring), low strength, estimated / detected enemy, stack / aggregate. Icons stay APP-6.

**Counter and marker tokens** (TASK-142, `unit_tokens_hex.html`, **variant D accepted 2026-11-11**, hexagon as base only; the extruded slab, a pin with a cloth flag, a glass plate and a miniature vehicle were rejected as cartoon-like or too heavy): a counter is a thin **hexagonal game tile** in matte resin, no outline. **The hexagon is only the base** (identical for both sides, flat-top, R 21 px): base = side colour lerp `ui.muted` 0.78 then lerp `map.void` 0.3 (neutral), wall 5 px × depth (base lerp black 0.5), recessed field R 17.8 (base lerp black 0.14), bevel of 6 lines (white 45 % on the upper-left edges, black 45 % on the lower-right). On it stands a **raised plate (1.6 px) that carries the side by shape and colour**: own = rectangle with the cut corner 24×16, enemy = diamond 23×23 (0.6 × the accepted counter shapes), fill `side.own` / `side.enemy` lerp neutral grey 0.2, 0.8 px white 35 % top edge. The APP-6 glyph is **engraved** on the plate (side colour lerp black 0.72 with a white 35 % edge 0.7 px low-right); the echelon bars are engraved on the base above the plate; 5 strength studs r 2, pitch 6.2 px, on the base under the plate (HP colours, `ui.alert` when low, unlit = dark recess); designation label right as before. Ground shadow (`StaffMapCounter.draw_shadow`, light from the upper left). **Pitch:** depth = `StaffMapCounter.depth_for_pitch`; vertical squash lerp(1, 0.8, depth), thickness and shadow × depth, so a flat map shows flat hexagons (no wall). States: hover = plate lightened 15 % + 1.2 px `ui.text` rim on the base; selected = 2 px `ui.amber` brackets around tile + studs; contact = dashed `ui.alert` ellipse on the ground; low = plate darkened 30 %, studs `ui.alert`; moved = plate darkened 40 %; estimated = 55 % opacity, '?', no studs; stack = up to 3 tiles stacked (+6.5 px each) with an amber count badge. **Installations** (depot, airfield, air defence, command post): the same base (R 18) with a chamfered square plate (0.46 × the counter size) carrying the engraved class glyph and 4 integrity studs; destroyed = grey plate with an `ui.alert` cross. No outlines except for states; no new tokens; no assets (polygons only).

**Orders:** posture = line style, picker `[1] March [2] Attack [3] Defend` under the selected formation (keys 1/2/3, ⌫ cancels); route smoothed with Chaikin ×3 through A* hex centres, halts shown as a cross-bar with "+n"; planning preview full opacity with hex dots and an amber target, issued orders 82 % without dots; enemy orders invisible (fog), intel may show a thin dotted red "predicted axis".

**Front line:** edge between an RU hex and a NATO hex on land, chained into polylines; smoothed variant (edge midpoints + Chaikin ×3) recommended, exact variant for debug / Z2 grid; teeth point to the enemy from the player's perspective (flip when playing Russia); rebuilt only on `control_changed`; no front against a closed country.

**Turn bar** (top centre): side + formation count, TURN number, date, day / night (odd turns 06–18, even 18–06) with sun / moon, side to move, End turn with `ORDERS n / m ↵` (greys out as "AI moving…"); under it the escalation chip (level, name, 5 ticks green → red; click opens the ladder).

**Panels:** formation panel on the right edge (strength, posture, order, supply, cohesion, location, subunits list); hex card at the cursor (`pointer-events: none`; elevation, road, river, country + stance, control, move cost, tags, route row only while planning); closed-country card (stance to you, access, forces estimate, opens at escalation level, entry cost). **Contact notice** next to the battle hex with a dimmed map: attacker vs defender, strength, odds, 3-band forecast (breakthrough / stalemate / repulse), Play battle `B` / Auto-resolve `A`, contact queue "1 OF 2" on the left. **After-action report:** who holds, retreat, strength before → after, losses own / enemy, front and escalation effect as separate lines, Continue `↵`. **Political layer:** escalation ladder table (0 Crisis … 4 Strategic threshold), decision card with expiry, 2–3 options with effects in one line, advisor. **Symmetry:** the view function `stance(country, player_side)` returns own / enemy / neutral / closed; everything else follows.

**Border warnings** (TASK-116, `border_warnings.html`; chamfered plates as the country card, 9 px cut, 1 px outline on the chamfers too): *escalation* (sanctuary breach) = variant B: 4 px `ui.amber` gate bar across the route at the border with a tag "▲ ESCALATION +1" and the sentence under it; when the incident reaches the top level (ends the operation) the bar, the tag and its edge turn `ui.alert` ("This ends the operation"). *Casus belli* = variant A: 26×23 px amber triangle with glyph "CB" + plate "NATO attacks out of Lithuania: casus belli for Russia" (chip "CB"). Plates: `map.void` 90 %, edge amber 45 %, Chakra Petch 12.5 bold; labels only in the planning preview, issued orders keep the markers (label on hover). **Confirmation** = variant A: 360 px plate above the hint line, header "CONFIRM ORDER · n INCIDENTS", one row per incident (chip + text), keys `↵ Confirm` (filled amber) / `Esc Cancel`, map dimmed 28 %, no timeout; edge `ui.alert` and a last row when an incident ends the operation. **Closed country** = variant A: 22 px `ui.alert` cross (2 × 3 px) in the hovered hex outline (1.6 px, 10 % fill), plate above "RUSSIA · CLOSED" + reason line (mono 11, `ui.muted`): "Sanctuary – opens at escalation 2 (Open war)", "Hostile – closed to your side", "Neutral – closed until it joins"; preview route dashed 3/5 up to the border; flips below the hex when it would reach the top 160 px (turn bar, escalation chip). Meaning is carried by shape and text, not only colour.

**Politics screen** (TASK-117, `political_decisions.html`, **variant B accepted 2026-11-10**; A left slide-out and C card hand rejected): a full-screen sheet (1300×780 px at 1440×900) over the dimmed map (`scrim.screen` rgba(0,0,0,.62)); decisions column on the left (five cards: key chip, title, cost chip LEVEL +1 / −1 / FREE, one-line effect, country chips with stance word and shape, reason line + Take; inline confirmation with the new level and a 5-tick ladder; amber BLOCKED / red ▲ THRESHOLD tags; disabled cards keep their position), a right column (~400 px) reserved for the read-only overview of TASK-119 (ladder table, countries list, event log – placeholders until then). **Escalation ladder window** (TASK-143, `escalation_ladder_window.html`, **variant B accepted 2026-11-11**; A vertical thermometer and C compact left column rejected): replaces the 460 px ladder card. Full overlay 1320×660 px at x 60 / y 90 (1440×900) over `scrim.screen`, plate `PANEL_STRONG`, chamfers 0,12,0,12, 1 px edge `#3a3f33`, tab "ESCALATION LADDER" on `level_colour(current)`, sub-line "Shared by both sides · level n of 6 · ordinary play stops at 5". **Horizontal axis**: 7 columns of 160 px (levels 0–6, left to right, label column 150 px): big level number (Chakra Petch 700 44), name (Chakra 700 13 caps), node on the axis line (reached = filled disc in `level_colour`, current = diamond with 2 px `ui.text` ring + amber chip "◆ NOW", above = hollow disc, level 6 = hollow diamond; line solid up to the current level, faint above, dashed `ui.alert` gate before level 6); the current column is framed `ui.amber` 8 % fill. Below the axis a **strike matrix** (rows Formations / Air defence / Depots / Airfields / Command posts): **tick ✓** = allowed, **✓ NEW** in an `ui.amber` frame = unlocked at this level, dashed "—" = not allowed, level 6 empty; then trigger chips per column (SANCTUARY OPENS at 2, ALLIES JOIN at 3, ▲ CAP at 5, ✕ END at 6 in `ui.alert`) with one line of text. Footer: per side (rectangle own / diamond enemy) strength gauge 10 px (`#b9d38a` ≥ 75 % / `#e2c069` ≥ 50 % / `ui.alert`) with a tick at the 50 % collapse line, "strength n % · readiness n", chip "▲ COLLAPSE" when in collapse. Hover or ←/→ focus on a node shows a 260 px plate with its condition (level 6: "level 5 · Russia in collapse · Russia's own decision"). **Closing**: X 28 px (as the politics sheet), Esc, click on the scrim; opening it closes every other staff-map window. Fade 150 ms, no timeout. No new tokens. Meaning by shape and text, not colour alone.

**Strike result report** (TASK-097b / 141b, `strike-result-report.html`, **variant A accepted 2026-11-10**; B drawer and C toast dropped): card in the notice frame (`PANEL_STRONG` rgba(8,11,8,.93), 1 px `LINE`, chamfers 0,16,0,16), 420 px wide, height by content (≈ 270–330 px), at x 16 / y 110 screen px (left edge under the turn-bar row); tab Chakra Petch 700 11 px on `side.own` / `side.enemy` (threshold: `ui.alert`), weapon name Chakra 700 21, designation / counts mono 600 11–13 (designation only once TASK-141a adds the field).
