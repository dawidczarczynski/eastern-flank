ACCEPTED: variant B (full Politics screen) · 2026-11-10 · TASK-117 · variants A and C are rejected alternatives

Political decisions on the staff map

The need. The five standing decisions of TASK-117 – Escalate, Stand down, Declare hostilities, Invoke alliance (named per side), Demand neutrality – must be readable as choices: what each does in one line, what it costs on the escalation ladder, whether it is available and, if not, why. The current small popup cramps this. Every variant shows, in all states: 5 cards (title, one-line effect, cost, Take), a country picker, a disabled state with reason (amber; red at the threshold), a confirmation showing the new level, the budget (“Decisions 1 of 1”), the stand-down counter, an X button + Esc. It closes by itself when the turn ends (fade 150 ms, no confirmation; an unconfirmed pick is dropped). Decisions are one per turn, so every card dims when the budget is 0.

Frames are crops of the 1440×900 layout (1:1 unless the caption gives a scale). Backdrop in the staff-map palette. Own = blue, enemy = red from either side’s point of view (Russia sees its own forces blue, as in the accepted mock-ups); only the side name and decision names change. Meaning is carried by shape and text, never colour alone: own = rectangle, enemy = diamond, neutral = circle; BLOCKED (amber) vs THRESHOLD (red, with triangle); cost chip text “LEVEL +1”.

States shown in each variant: S1 NATO, turn start, level 1 (mixed available / blocked, picker open) · S2 confirming Escalate (1 → 2) · S3 Russia at level 3: threshold (red), recent escalation, level too high (amber) · S4 NATO after taking a decision: result line, budget 0 of 1, stand-down counter 2 turns.
