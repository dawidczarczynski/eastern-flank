Border warnings on the staff map

Three elements TASK-116 needs and the mockups in operational_map/ do not show: (1) a warning marker + label at the first hex of a route incident, (2) the confirmation before an order that causes an incident, (3) the "closed country" cross with its reason. Each has variants A/B/C; frames are 560×300 crops at 1:1 of the 1440×900 layout, backdrop in the staff-map palette (map.land, border dash-dot, route in ui.amber, own counter 40×26). Everything is screen-space, fixed size at every zoom. Meaning never rests on colour alone: shape + text carry it.

Wording fix included: Russia opens at escalation 2 = Open war (code level 2 = Open war; the manual numbers levels from 1).

1 · Route warning marker

Appears at the first hex of each incident (route_incidents): sanctuary breach (+n escalation), casus belli, and any incident that reaches the top level ("ends the operation"). Shown in the planning preview with a label; on issued orders the marker stays, the label shows on hover.

Recommendation and open questions

1A + 2A + 3A: same chamfered plate, chips and amber/alert language as the existing cards, text carries the meaning, confirmation is a clear blocking step. 1B is the best alternative if you want less text on the map.

  • New tokens: none (all colours are existing StaffMapPalette / HUD v3 values). Only new shape: the 26×23 triangle (the chamfered plate and chips reuse the country-card look).
  • Q1: should the confirmation also be required for casus belli alone, or only for sanctuary breach? (Proposed: both – both change the political state.)
  • Q2: hatch the closed country when a formation is selected (3B), or only on hover (3A)?
