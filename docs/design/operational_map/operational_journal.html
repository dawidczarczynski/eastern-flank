Operational journal – a per-side log of the operation

1 · The need

Today the only "log" is the strike card queue (J), the turn report (only the last move of the viewing side) and the political event list. The player has no place to read what happened to my side, turn by turn. The journal collects, for the viewing side only, every event that touched it: strikes (own and incoming), battles, formation movement and halts, retreats and losses, installations hit, political changes. Entries are grouped under a turn header with its date and day / night, newest turn first, written from the side's point of view (our / enemy, never NATO / Russia).

  • Opened from a new bottom bar button JRN (key J; strike cards keep their own queue and open the journal at the strike entry with their "Log" hint). Docked left window, one at a time, like Report / Strike / Map options.
  • Filter: ALL · STRIKES · BATTLES · MOVES · POLITICS (chips, one active). Default ALL.
  • Visibility (human decision 2026-11-12): own actions in full; the enemy only where it touched us (a strike or battle against us, an enemy halted in contact with our formation). Enemy moves far away are not written. No fog of war yet, the same rule will carry over to TASK-100.
  • Side is carried by shape + word, not colour alone: own = rectangle, enemy = diamond, neutral / political = circle. The tag column (STR · BTL · MOV · POL) names the category.
  • Click on an entry with a place centres the map on it (as report rows do) and rings the hex for 2 s. Hover = white 7 %.
  • Must not hide: the turn bar row, the selected counter, the route. It does not block map input.
  • States: normal, filtered, empty ("Nothing has happened yet. Orders you issue and strikes you launch are recorded here."), new entries (current turn header carries NOW; unseen entries since the last close have a 3 px amber left bar until hovered), hover, capped (older than 20 turns are dropped, footer "Older entries are not kept").

5 · Decisions (human, 2026-11-12)

  1. J opens the journal; the strike cards open it at the strike entry with Enter or a click on the chip (the card queue keeps ◂ ▸ and X).
  2. All entries are kept for the whole operation (no cap).
  3. Political entries are bare facts (effects only, never who decided), as PoliticalLog.

New tokens: none. Date format "14 JUN" comes from OperationalClock (12 h per turn; DAY = odd turns 06–18).
