Strike result report – staff map

Today a deep strike ends in a one-line toast (StaffMapStrikeText.resolved): "Cruise ×2 on Chernyakhovsk airfield: 1 intercepted, 1 hit – integrity 100 → 65". It drops the interceptor sites, the stock, the escalation change and does not tell the player what a missile did. Needed: a readable report after every strike, own (immediately) and enemy (during its turn). It must carry a named weapon plus designation (TASK-141: "Iskander-M · 9K720", "JASSM-ER · AGM-158B", "Tomahawk Block V · RGM-109E"), never cover the selected counter, the route or the strike panel (right edge, 330 px, 16 px margin) or the confirm card (centre, 22 % height), and never use colour as the only carrier of meaning.

ElementSpec
Missile pictogram (one per missile, 24 px; 15 px in compact rows) HIT solid amber disc with a dark ✕   INTERCEPTED blue-grey ring with a bar through it   MISSED dashed empty muted ring. Fill / stroke pattern differs, so it survives colour-blindness; counts and the words HIT / INTERCEPTED / MISSED are always printed too. Order in the row: hits, intercepted, missed.
Sideown = blue rectangle 14×9, enemy = red diamond 10×10 (the counter shapes) in the tab / left bar, plus the words YOUR STRIKE / ENEMY STRIKE and NATO / RUSSIA.
TargetInstallation: "INTEGRITY b → a", bar with a hatched ghost for the lost part and −Δ in ui.alert; bar colour by the strength thresholds (≥75 green, ≥50 yellow, else ui.alert). Destroyed: bar fully hatched with outline, tag "✕ DESTROYED". Formation: "STRENGTH b → a / 20" with the 5 slanted segments of the counter; lost segments hatched.
Escalation5 ticks as in the escalation chip, "▲ +n" in amber (rises) or ui.alert (new level ≥ 3), "a → b · LEVEL NAME", then one line per cause. At the top level the plate edge, tab and a "▲ OPERATION ENDS" tag turn ui.alert. Reaching a level with no change prints "NO CHANGE".
Header fitWeapon name: Chakra Petch 700 21 px caps, one line, ellipsis at ~26 characters ("TOMAHAWK BLOCK V" fits with room). Designation + class + salvo on a second line in JetBrains Mono 11 caps: designation ui.amber, the rest ui.muted. The weapon name is the display_name of the means, the designation a new data field (open question 1).
FogAn enemy strike shows what the viewer could observe: outcome counts, target, damage and escalation; no stock / strikes left (shows "unknown"). Interceptors only for own air-defence sites or sites the viewer sees (TASK-100 hook is_visible). Designation (e.g. 9K720) shown only once TASK-141a adds the field; until then the header shows the name only.

Godot notes: new StaffMapStrikeReport extends FramedPanel fed by a pure model StaffMapStrikeText.report_model(state, event, trail) (dictionary: header, outcomes, target, interceptors, escalation, stock), so it is testable without a scene; keep resolved() as the one-line fallback / accessibility string. Reuse chamfers, tab_color, line_color exactly as StaffMapStrikeConfirm; pictograms drawn in _draw (circle + two lines, no textures); ticks from StaffMapEscalationChip; owner is StaffMapStrikeHud (it already receives the events and the trail). Escalation lines come from the existing escalation events (escalation_changed, sanctuary_breached) emitted by the same command.
