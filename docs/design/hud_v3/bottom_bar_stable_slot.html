Stable height of the left slot

Need. Since the unit strip shares the terrain strip's slot, the bar jumps when the cursor goes from a hex to a unit: the terrain strip is a tab and a one-line 17 px title (~44 px tall) and the unit strip is a tab and a two-line name (~52 px) plus 8 px padding either side, so the bar grows from the accepted 76 px to 80 px (measured on screenshots: top edge at y 644 vs 640 of 720). Wanted: no movement of the bar, of the divider, of the tab row, whichever strip (or the empty state) is shown. Must not hide: centre icons, ready counter, End turn.

Every frame is the same 76 px crop, so the tab row and the divider stay at the same y when you compare them. Tokens used are all existing (--amber, --own, --enemy, --line, --muted, --ok = HP_HIGH). No new tokens.

The terrain title is one 17 px line that sits in the 34 px title block (city line below it when there is one); the unit name is up to two 12 px lines in the same block.
