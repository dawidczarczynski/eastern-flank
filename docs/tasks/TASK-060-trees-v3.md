# TASK-060: Trees v3 – leaf-card crowns with trunks and branches, wind, per-instance variation

- **Status**: idea – **decision needed**: option A (procedural) vs. option B (CC0 glTF models), and the tree size from TASK-059
- **Layers**: presentation (`foliage_meshes.gd` or imported models, `assets/shaders/foliage.gdshader`, `map_view.gd` scatter), tests
- **Depends on**: **TASK-059** (tree scale decided), **TASK-057** (shared wind include and global uniforms), soft **TASK-050** (`wind`, LOD distances), soft **TASK-030** (`MeshKit.lathe`/`rod` for trunks and branches, option A); option B also **TASK-053** (models allowed)
- **Model**: opus (leaf-card generation with correct normals for foliage lighting, LOD/visibility ranges per chunk, alpha-scissor shadows, performance)

## Goal

Forests look like real woodland: individual trees with visible trunks and branches, crowns made of many leaf clusters with soft, light-transmitting edges that move in the wind, and enough variation in shape and colour that no two neighbours look alike.

## Current state

`FoliageMeshes`: deciduous crowns = 5 lumpy icosphere lobes, conifers = 4 stacked cones, baked AO in vertex colours, bent normals; uniform yellow-green instance colours (`DECIDUOUS_COLOR_A/B`, `CONIFER_COLOR_A/B`); 2 variants each; no wind; one `MultiMeshInstance3D` per mesh type for the whole map.

## Open questions / decision needed (human)

- **Option A – procedural (keeps "everything procedural")**: trunk + 3–6 main branches built with `MeshKit.lathe`/`rod` (taper); crown = **60–120 alpha-scissor leaf cards** (quads 0.04–0.07 units) placed on the existing lobe spheres, facing outward with random roll; leaf-cluster texture generated by a pure `LeafTexture.cluster(size, seed)` (overlapping leaf silhouettes, alpha scissor 0.5, slight colour variation); conifers = drooping card skirts along the trunk with a needle texture. Card normals = crown-centre radial (bent, as today) so crowns light as volumes. Pro: no assets, consistent with the unit pipeline. Con: realism ceiling depends on the generator; more agent time.
- **Option B – CC0 glTF trees** (e.g. Poly Haven, Quaternius, other CC0 sets): 3–4 deciduous (birch, oak, beech-like) and 2 conifer (pine, spruce) species typical for Poland/Western Russia, each with LOD0/LOD1, re-materialised with our foliage shader. Pro: much higher realism quickly. Con: breaks "everything procedural" (needs ADR 0009 to allow models), style must match, import/size budget, licence check per model.
- Species mix: proposed pine/spruce on high ground (today's conifer rule) and birch/oak/alder elsewhere.

## Design (common to A and B)

- **Foliage shader** `assets/shaders/foliage.gdshader`: alpha scissor (0.5), `cull_disabled` for leaves, **back-lighting/transmission** (`BACKLIGHT` ≈ 0.35 with leaf colour), bent normals from the mesh, AO from vertex colour, wind from the shared include (TASK-057): trunk sway `∝ h²` (small), leaf flutter (per-card phase from vertex colour/UV2), both scaled by `wind_strength`.
- **Per-instance variation** (`INSTANCE_CUSTOM`): hue shift ±0.03, value ×0.85–1.1, saturation ×0.9–1.05, chosen from the plan's `k` and a second hash; autumn-ish odd tree 1 in 25 (slightly yellower) – starting values, tune on screenshots.
- **Chunks + LOD**: trees grouped into MultiMesh chunks (same 4×4-unit chunks as grass or 8×8); LOD0 (cards) within **30** units of the camera, LOD1 (today's lobed crown mesh, recoloured and with the same variation) beyond, using `visibility_range_begin/end` with fade margins of 4. Shadows: LOD0 casts alpha-scissor shadows on HIGH; LOD1 casts on LOW.
- At least **3 variants** per tree type.
- Placement and counts stay `ScatterPlanner`'s (only meshes, sizes per TASK-059, and materials change).

## Acceptance criteria (testable)

- [ ] (to be written when the task becomes `ready`, after the decision) e.g. option A: `build_deciduous_cards(v)` is deterministic, variant 0 ≠ 1; card count in [60, 120]; every card normal points away from the crown centre (`dot > 0` for ≥ 95 %); the trunk mesh is closed at the top below the crown; bounds follow the TASK-059 tree scale. Both options: LOD0/LOD1 visibility ranges 30 ± margin 4; instance custom data in the documented ranges; with `wind = false` the wind strength uniform is 0; scatter tests unchanged.

## Playtest (for the human)

1. Zoom in on a forest: trunks and branches visible, crowns made of leaf clusters, edges soft and slightly see-through against the sky.
2. Watch: leaves flutter and trees sway a little, not in sync.
3. Neighbouring trees differ in shape and shade; a few slightly yellower ones.
4. Zoom out: no visible switch between the detailed and the simple trees.
5. Frame rate over the largest forest at full zoom-in (Windows) and at LOW (`tools/run.sh`).
