## Goal

The two rocket launchers look like used steel trucks instead of box kits when the player zooms in or looks at a unit portrait – rounded edges that catch the light, bolts, hinges, handles, hydraulic lines, tyres with tread and hubs with bolt rings, jacks and shields with real hardware – while they read exactly as before from the battle camera, animate their launcher, jacks and shields exactly as before, and cost no frame rate and no noticeable hitch at battle start.

## Design

- **Kit and paint**: build both trucks through `MeshKits` (one kit per material role per pivot; ADR 0012) – hull, `Launcher`, `Launcher/Pod`, each actuator part, each `Stabiliser<i>` / `BlastShield<i>` and the wheels. No own kit copies, no extra shader: the paint is TASK-062's `camo.gdshader`; per-material wear / dust / mud parameters (more dust and mud low on the chassis and tyres, worn edges on steps, bumpers and pod corners) go into the truck materials as shader parameters. Missing primitives go into `MeshKit` with a test in `test_mesh_kit.gd`, not into the model files. Faction-independent geometry is cached once for all factions (`ModelParts.cached_meshes`).
- **Shared wheeled running gear** (presentation-engineer, new part next to `TrackedRunningGear`, reusing its wheel techniques): a `Spec` with axle positions, wheel radius, tyre width, track (`z`), tread pattern (directional off-road lugs as chamfered blocks on the visible outer shoulder and crown), hub style (dished steel disc with a bolt ring and hub cap; option for a central tyre-inflation hub as on the ZIL-135 and Jelcz), plain inner half (a simple drum, no lugs or bolts), and opt-in mudflaps / wheel-arch liners. One wheel mesh set per side is built once and shared by every wheel pivot of that side (build time). Builds the same `Wheel<i>` pivots (name, position, spin about local Z, count 8) as today, so `UnitModels.pose_tank` / locomotion and the `wheels` mechanism contract keep working. `ModelParts.wheel` stays as it is for any other caller (do not edit `model_parts.gd` while TASK-067's changes are uncommitted; if a helper there must change, wait for 067 to be committed).
- **Homar-K** (Jelcz P882.57 + K239 module): keep `K`, `LENGTH_M`, the axle positions, `POD_*`, `TURNTABLE_*` and actuator constants. Armoured cab-over cab as chamfered plates (visible armour seams, door outlines with hinges and handles, thick window frames, wipers, mirrors on arms, grab handles, steps with tread plates, headlight guards, antenna bases); equipment block with ribbed air-filter drums and clamps; exhaust stack with a heat shield; lockers with hinges and latches; frame rails and cross members seen between the wheel groups; launcher module: two pods under the flat cover with chamfered edges, the dark centre seam, lifting eyes, cover rivets / seams, rear tube caps as shallow lathes with rims; turntable with a bolt ring; actuators as chamfered cylinders with end eyes and a hydraulic line (`tube`). The faction accent plates and pod band stay where they are.
- **9K57 Uragan** (9P140 on ZIL-135LM): keep `K`, `LENGTH_M`, axles, arches, `TURNTABLE`, `HINGE`, tube constants. Cab with the raked windscreen in thick frames, the cab-front grille, headlights with guards, doors with hinges; twin engine compartment with grilles as slats, three arched fenders as smooth swept plates; 16 tubes as lathes with visible open muzzles (dark bore a short way in, then closed), clamp bands with bolts, the stepped muzzle frame, cradle beams and trunnions, hydraulic lines along the cradle; turntable ring with bolts. The tubes are where the camera looks – they get the triangles, the tube bundle's inner tubes (hidden by the outer ones) get the simplest shape (fewer sides, no rims).
- **Deploy parts (from TASK-045, rebuilt, not redesigned)**: `Stabiliser<i>` jacks as chamfered housings with an inner leg, a round foot pad and a hydraulic line; `BlastShield<i>` (Uragan) as chamfered armour plates with hinge knuckles and a stiffener rib. Pivot names, positions, `deployed` meta and motion stay exactly as TASK-045 built them; only the geometry under the pivots is replaced.
- **Launcher pivots (Barrel-like rule from TASK-069)**: everything that elevates is under `Launcher/Pod` (tubes / pod box, caps, muzzle frame, clamp bands, cradle, `ActuatorMount<i>`), everything that only traverses is under `Launcher` (turntable top, upper carriage, girders, `Actuator<i>` / `ActuatorRod<i>`), nothing of the launcher sits in the hull meshes except the fixed turntable base. `Mouth<i>` markers (12 / 16) stay one per tube, on the tube axis just beyond its end, so the salvo still leaves from the tube ends. Each truck declares `LAUNCHER_CLEARANCE_H` (metres: the highest hull / cab / module point inside the raised pod's swept circle) for the sweep test below. Traverse range, stowed direction and elevation come from the unit's `MECHANISMS` / `UnitModels.launcher_params` (TASK-044) – this task changes none of them.
- **Small parts** (both, where the camera sees them): bolts and hinges on doors, hatches and lockers, grab handles and steps, tow hooks and shackles, hydraulic and air lines (`tube`), jerrycan / stowage brackets, lights with guards, antenna bases.
- **Hidden geometry cut** (ADR 0010 rule): inner wheel halves as plain drums, no lugs on the inner tyre shoulders, frame and axles behind the wheels as the simplest shapes, inner tubes of the Uragan bundle simplified, nothing inside the cab, the equipment block or the Homar-K pod box, no faces under parts that sit flush on another part. Each truck declares `HULL_INTERIOR: Array[AABB]` (vehicle metres: x from the front, h, z) – boxes strictly inside closed shells: the cab (both, at least 1.5 m × 0.6 m × 1.6 m) and, for the Homar-K, the pod box (at least 3.0 × 0.8 × 2.0 m, in `Pod` space at rest) – used by the hidden-geometry test.
- **Anchors** (TASK-064 pass): `Exhaust` (Homar-K: top of the exhaust stack, ~x 4.47, h 3.3, z +0.9; Uragan: the exhaust outlet from its references) and `Dust_L` / `Dust_R` (ground level behind the rear axle's tyres) as `Marker3D` children of `Model`. If TASK-063 is done first, also its `Marking_*` / `Number_*` anchors per its spec.
- **Budget**: ≤ 40 000 triangles per truck for every faction, ≤ 24 body mesh instances, cold build ≤ 500 ms (contract) with a target of ≤ 300 ms each. If a truck goes over, cut detail where the camera never looks first (inner tyre halves, tubes inside the bundle, chassis underside, lathe sides of small parts), never the silhouette.
- **Silhouette and scale unchanged**: same `K`, same origin, same `LABEL_HEIGHT`; the battle-camera read must not change.

## Playtest (for the human)

1. Start a battle: no noticeable hitch when the first Homar-K and Uragan appear (compare with before).
2. Zoom in on a Homar-K: armoured cab with rounded plate edges, door hinges and handles, steps, mirrors; air-filter drums and exhaust stack; pod cover with chamfered edges and seams; tyres with lugs and hubs with bolts; worn paint on corners, dust low on the chassis.
3. Zoom in on an Uragan: raked windscreen in thick frames, smooth arched fenders, 16 tubes with open muzzles and clamp bands, the muzzle frame, hydraulic lines on the cradle.
4. Orbit the camera around both, low and high: no holes or see-through gaps where hidden parts were removed (look at the wheel backs from the front, under the chassis from a low angle, into the Uragan's tube bundle), no flickering.
5. Move both trucks: wheels spin, dust comes from behind the rear wheels, exhaust from the Homar-K's stack and the Uragan's outlet.
6. Fire with both: jacks go down (and the Uragan's shields close), the pod rises and traverses without passing through the truck, the rockets leave from the tube ends, everything returns – same sequence and timing as before.
7. Zoom out to the normal battle camera: both trucks read the same as before (silhouette, size, camo).
8. Hover a launcher for the unit popup portrait: the detail shows there.
9. FPS counter with all units on screen: still 60 FPS.
