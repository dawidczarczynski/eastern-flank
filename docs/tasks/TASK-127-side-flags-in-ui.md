# TASK-127: Side flags in the UI – a flag next to each side name, first in the battle result notice

- **Status**: done
- **Layers**: data (`SideDefinition.flag`, flag assets) + presentation
- **Depends on**: – (TASK-120 is done: the battle result notice with side tabs exists)
- **Sections**: none (UI only; look = HUD v3, `docs/design/hud_v3`)
- **Model**: sonnet
- **Topic**: UI

## Goal

A small flag appears wherever the staff map names a side, so the sides are recognised at a glance. First place: the battle result notice (`StaffMapBattleResult`) – next to the "Victory: <side>" headline and on each side's tab (requested by the human 2026-11-09); then the staff map turn bar and the contact notice. One flag per **side** (`nato`, `russia`); per-country flags inside a bloc are out of scope.

## Design

- Data: `SideDefinition` gets `@export var flag: Texture2D` (rule 5: the flag belongs to the side's data); `data/sides/nato.tres` and `russia.tres` set it. Assets in `assets/flags/` (`nato.svg`, `russia.svg`), **own simple drawings only**: NATO = dark blue field with a white four-pointed compass star; Russia = white / blue / red horizontal tricolour. No copied images, no licence trouble. Import them with `godot --headless --import` (the `.import` files are generated, not hand-written).
- One shared component `SideFlag` (`src/presentation/ui/side_flag.gd`, `extends Control`): a flat flag of fixed aspect 3:2 drawn from a texture, with a 1 px frame in the side colour (HUD v3 frame look: `UiPalette.LINE` when no colour is given). API: `setup(side: SideDefinition, frame_colour: Color)`; hidden (`visible` false, zero size) when the side is null or has no flag. Size presets `SMALL` 18 × 12 (tabs, outcome lines, top bar) and `LARGE` 30 × 20 (result headline).
- Reuse, don't re-draw: every place calls `SideFlag`; no place loads the textures itself.

## Acceptance criteria (testable)

- [ ] `SideDefinition.flag` exists; both shipped sides have one; a test loads every `data/sides/*.tres` and checks `flag` is a non-null texture (a future side without a flag is a test failure, so it is added on purpose).
- [ ] `SideFlag.setup(side, colour)`: shows the texture at the preset size with the frame in `colour`; `setup(null, …)` and a side with `flag == null` leave it hidden and not taking layout space (`visible` false). One test each.
- [ ] Result notice headline: the title row holds a `SideFlag` (LARGE, name `TitleFlag`) before the "Victory: <side>" text for the winning side; hidden for a draw. Test with a result won by player 0 → flag visible, texture = the winner's side flag; draw → hidden.
- [ ] Result notice side tabs: each tab shows its side's flag (SMALL) before the formation name, in the same colour as the tab text; the pressed tab keeps the flag readable (frame in the ink colour). Test: two tabs, each with the flag of its own player's side (attacker first).
- [ ] Result notice outcome strip ("<side> holds the field" / "<side> retreats"): the SMALL flag precedes each line.
- [ ] Staff map turn bar: the SMALL flag sits next to the side name in each side block (the hex badge stays); follows the side colour/alpha of the block (active / inactive). Test: two blocks, each flag = its side's.
- [ ] Contact notice: the SMALL flag precedes the "<side> · <role>" line of each of the two formations.
- [ ] No layout regression: the result notice keeps `WIDTH` 540 and the existing tests of `StaffMapBattleResult`, turn bar and contact notice pass unchanged.
- [ ] 60 FPS budget: flags are plain `TextureRect`-class draws (no shader, no per-frame redraw); SVGs are rasterised once at import.
- [ ] `docs/CODEMAP.md` is regenerated by `tools/check.sh`; `tools/readme_screenshot.sh` re-run only if the staff map top bar is part of it (check with Read).

## Playtest (for the human)

1. Start the Suwałki operation, bring a Polish battalion into contact with a Russian one, press Auto-resolve (or play and report).
2. In the result notice: the NATO or Russia flag stands left of "Victory: …" (none on a draw); each side tab has its flag; the "holds the field / retreats" lines have flags.
3. Staff map top bar: each side block shows its flag next to the side name; the inactive side's flag is dimmed with the block.
4. Contact notice before a battle: each formation's "NATO · ATTACKER" / "RUSSIA · DEFENDER" line has the right flag.
5. Flags look flat and small with a thin frame, not blurry (check at 1080p and 1440p); the NATO flag is a blue field with a white star, the Russian one a tricolour.

## Player manual

None (cosmetic); at most one sentence in the staff map UI section of docs/MANUAL.md if the top bar is described there.

## Out of scope

- Per-country or per-faction flags inside a bloc, flags in the unit catalog, the tactical HUD, the politics panel, the escalation notice (follow-up once `SideFlag` exists – each is a one-line `setup` call).
- Animated flags, flag selection by the player, additional sides.
